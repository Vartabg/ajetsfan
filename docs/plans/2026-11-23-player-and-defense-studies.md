# Read the defense, find the player, follow the safety

Revision 1 against `36756b5`. The owner accepted three experiences for fans who want to understand football without the video. Keep the established Lexend focus layout and the 22-player replay. The earlier isolated throw diagram was rejected. Fans should pick a charted defense and compare two real throws, find a player's plays in a football situation, and see why a safety entered a game for one snap. The prototype is the existing Cleveland and Buffalo boards at third-and-10, three Allen conversions on one Chargers drive, and Davis's gamebook play. Check a working control for each before refining the composition.
