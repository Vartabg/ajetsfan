# Situational football on ajetsfan

Revision 1, based on 65c62f3. The owner wants to explore what the Jets do with the ball, what their defense gives up, and when it changes. The accepted interactions are to compare third-down target depth, compare four and five rushers on the same down and distance, and pick a fourth-down call before seeing its modeled win probabilities and actual result. Team Stats must also show key situations, changes over time and the red zone. Keep the exact-play reader, the full-team board, source boundaries and the player leaders.

## On the site

Make Team Stats an entry point where a fan selects a season and offense or defense, reads in ordinary football language what happened, compares the whole regular season with its last four games, and opens actual supporting plays. Show a small number of meaningful rates with counts and football units. Include third-down conversion, touchdowns per evidenced red-zone trip, early-down play calling, and quarter or clock splits, including the last two minutes of either half and a one-score fourth quarter. Give the time splits their own selectable view or rows and show what the team actually ran and gained there. Keep player leaders available with their original anchors. Retain the existing focus typography and palette, side-by-side labels that stack on phones, short horizontal comparison marks when useful, and ordinary links and forms for keyboard and script-disabled use.

In Game replays and Team Stats link three sibling studies. A fan can select either third-down depth group or either rusher count, see numerator, denominator and sample scope, open a contributing play in the existing Xs & Os reader, or step through the selected sample across games. The pass-rush study fixes both down and exact distance for its two cohorts, shows sacks and first downs surrendered, and calls the sample charted opponent pass plays and sacks. The target-depth study counts named Jets third-down attempts with affirmative knowledge of depth, positive distance and adjudicated result. Equal to the line to gain belongs to at-or-beyond. Show the observed conversion percentage and how many plays it comes from. Unknown outcomes never become failures. No causal interpretation or unadjusted league comparison.

Present fourth down as a pre-play situation with the Jets score, clock, possession, down, distance and spot. Choose go, punt or field goal through a native URL. After the choice show each available expected win probability for the Jets offense, the coach’s actual decision and recorded result, then link to the play and a fresh decision. Use only real nfl4th probabilities joined to the same verified game and play. The upstream nfl4th precomputed release includes go_wp, punt_wp and fg_wp; verify units, release license and version, and retain reproducible source/asset identity. For a three-choice game include only rows with all three finite probabilities and an identifiable actual Jets fourth-down decision. Exclude administrative rows, fake-vs-kick ambiguity, complex rulings and unsupported states unless the adjudicated action can be confirmed. Retain unrounded values for comparison, present close estimates at sensible precision and do not use post-play win probability to value an unplayed alternative. The initial HTML, metadata, navigation prefetches and component payload must be free of the coach's call, play description and all three estimates. Use stable game/play identity, canonical shareable URLs, server-side filtering and useful empty responses. Do not tell fans the outcome in the next decision link.

## Evidence and limits

Reuse published fixture matching, validated snap season packs, possession/period/clock and team labels. Use verified regular-season fixtures to choose Team Stats’ most recent four before assessing replay availability. State covered vs selected games and the through-date so a missing pack or recent fixture is visible. Use season from game ID for January. Do not count unplayed games, kneels/spikes as designed runs/passes, kicks as offensive scrimmage plays, null clocks as final minutes or a missing score as tied. For red-zone trips group once by game, drive and offense with a scrimmage snap at the opponent 20 or closer. Verify the scoring unit/outcome, exclude unresolvable trip outcomes and link the entry or finish snap. Name the operational definition and any data exclusions. When the denominator is zero report unavailable with a count; a measured zero has a real denominator. Never infer player tracks. Reuse licensed nflverse participation records with season-specific attribution for pass rush.

Use small server-projected tables and one selected cutup. Share the existing charted game, snap and verified season loaders; avoid copying all plays into the browser or every route. Add trace support only to routes that need those archives. Give every sample deterministic ordering, addressable play IDs, bounded result pages, and explicit no-match states. Do not transmit fourth-down outcomes on setup. Keep the existing performance budgets. Test red-zone repeat snaps, turnovers and defensive scores, unknown outcomes, tied games, quarter boundaries, last four including missing games, exact-distance equality, missing charting, fourth-down decision identity, no-spoiler payload, source values, navigation, native input and responsive rendering. Verify published entry points in production after the checks pass.

Revision 2. The graded three-choice fourth-down game is limited to NFLverse Jets offensive possessions with all three model estimates and a credited action. State this coverage at the point of choice. In the inspected November release, punt estimates stop at the opponent’s 30. Use an example in the region where all three alternatives are meaningful and link the model source and limitations. Pass-rush comparisons request participation only for supported 2016-onward seasons, show actual matched coverage, and project small counts and a bounded list on the server. In early-season or incomplete Team Stats windows show games covered out of the actual confirmed fixture count (up to four). Link only verified plays. The default archive readers support schematic full teams on ordinary plays; exact participant names remain evidence-dependent.

The first interaction proof exposed a change in depth coverage. The 2006–08 packs report air yards for completions alone, producing a misleading perfect conversion rate for measured targets beyond the sticks. Start comparative throw samples in 2009 and explain this at the table.

The source audit found that penalties can leave an official scoring play or third-down decision without a clean diagram record. Read the paired nflverse third-down flags and credited scrimmage and scoring fields independently of the diagram. Keep the original conservative drawing rules. Show recent red-zone trips in game order, with each result linked to its archived play. Use a clock selector so phone readers can compare a quarter, the final two minutes or a one-score fourth quarter without scrolling through six tables.
