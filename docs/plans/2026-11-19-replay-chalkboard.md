# Xs and Os for the selected snap

Revision 1 against 8122c71. Fans have accepted the game replay and now want to see the football play in Xs and Os. On the selected play, show who acted, where the ball went and what the charting tells us about the formation or defense. The game controls still advance or reverse the whole view.

## Interaction and evidence

Place a two-dimensional coaching board under the play account in game replay and in the exact-play evidence section. Offense attacks up on this board. The separate full-field graphic keeps its established Jets-left orientation. O denotes offense and X denotes defense. On kicks O is the kicking unit and X the receiving unit. The graphic opens on Play action. Native At the snap and Play action controls switch diagrams at the same clock, with a usable static rendering, keyboard selection, visible focus, textual equivalents and no automatic animation. Reset to action on a different selected play. Use a quiet field with line of scrimmage and clear player and event markers. Distinguish the drawn path of a throw, a carry after the catch and an incomplete or intercepted pass. Show negative plays and loss of possession correctly. A kickoff can show the kicker, receiver/returner and a credited tackle.

Where both units and formation are charted, show a conventional arrangement of their recorded personnel groups with clearly labeled schematic spacing. Never turn a coverage category into assigned defenders, a pass location into a receiver's alignment, a tackle into a coverage matchup or a yardage total into a tracked route. A charted primary route can have its own labeled concept sketch, independent of the named target and its ball path. On Wilson's uniquely matched touchdown, retain the known disagreement between the post and slant labels and link to the existing detailed study. Selective charting, including zero rushers, stays useful independently. With play-by-play only, draw the recorded actors and available direction with a visible basis label. A complex ruling or missing evidence gives a short reason and the recorded account. A single-play result never reveals a later snap or game outcome.

## Data and implementation

Extend the existing snap compiler and validator with optional play-by-play facts for direction, gap, credited defenders and routine kicks. Only use compatible, adjudicated source fields. Preserve old packs and omit ambiguous events. Keep licensed participation in compact, independently buildable season files. Use the published 2016–25 nflverse participation releases as available. Match a unique game and play and the team possessing the ball before adding a row's formation, personnel, primary route, coverage or rusher count. Read one applicable season for an individual verified game; the directory and all-season comparison can keep reading their ordinary play snapshots. Absence or malformed data must leave the game usable. Show NFL Next Gen Stats via nflverse through 2022 and FTN Data via nflverse since 2023, with CC BY-SA 4.0 and source links for that derivative. No charting lookups in the visitor's browser. The authenticated publication, current TV reader, key-play selection, URL and replay deck contracts continue through their existing entry points. Keep inert football diagrams out of site-wide bundles.

Implement a small pure view model and shared SVG component within the existing typography and controls. Reuse supported source facts and existing film work; loading a 3D scene or editable playbook for every snap would send unrelated models. Render variable player groups only when their supported counts make a valid unit. Treat diagram geometry and routes as schematic; record which facts support the result in adjacent text.
