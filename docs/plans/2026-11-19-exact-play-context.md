# Explain a Jets play to a fan arriving mid-game

Revision 1 against `963ff5c` on the open snap pilot. The owner wants the football basics on the exact-play page and asks for deeper play-level insight. A fan should be able to tell a friend who had the ball, where they were, who was ahead, what happened, and why it mattered. Keep the one-play-at-a-time interaction and the shareable TV clock. The owner found the first version too sparse. There is no new preference about colors or branding. The five-fan study stays ready for later.

An event log emphasizes sequence but forces the reader to reconstruct the score and field position. A compact broadcast scoreboard with a plain-language play account answers the immediate question, so use that as the first view. A separate section below it lets an analyst investigate the same snap. Retain the site's Lexend typography, flat layout, keyboard navigation and native links.

## Reader

- Identify the teams and venue relationship, date, period and time remaining. Clearly label the provider clock on ESPN rows. Put a compact scoreboard, pre-snap offense, down and distance, and named team yard line together above the play controls. Show the score entering the play and the resulting lead, tie or score change with the correct time label. Include each team's recorded timeouts when the source supplies them. Show a missing result score honestly, including grouped ESPN scoring rows.
- Translate the football situation into ordinary language. A third-and-10 at the Cleveland 15 with 25 seconds left and the Jets trailing 30–24 leads into Joe Flacco's tying touchdown pass to Garrett Wilson. Buffalo's first-and-10 from its 38 leads into Josh Allen's completion to Dalton Kincaid at the Jets 8. On Cleveland's intercepted pass, distinguish the Browns' offense at the snap and the Jets' takeaway. Give ordinary plays meaningful action titles and source-based accounts. Preserve the full play record in a disclosure, including penalties and reviews. Special teams and administrative plays receive their own labels.
- A compact schematic shows the starting ball spot and line to gain for eligible scrimmage plays, with a distinct finish only for reliably placed ordinary gains. Use the same offense-left-to-right orientation for either team, give textual equivalents, and handle midfield, goal-to-go, gains/losses and missing evidence. Kicks, penalties, turnovers, laterals and ambiguous spots do not inherit a fictional offensive advance.
- Under the opening state, present a short reading of the football numbers. Show offensive EPA with its perspective and a concise definition, the pre-snap model probability of a dropback, and the completion model's estimate for an actual target when available. On simple completions distinguish air yards from yards after catch. Rank the absolute recorded Jets win-probability change among the measured plays through this snap, with the population and tie handling clear. Never pull the next play, game final, final drive result or later model value into the readout.
- Extend the existing DuckDB season compiler and Snap type with optional, validated facts from the same recorded row. Older season files stay readable. Use direct source fields and preserve zero, negative air yards, unknowns and approved source timing. Keep model quantities separate from observed football actions. The live reader uses existing ESPN fields and its sanitized text, with extra fields only when trustworthy. Do not infer advanced stats from ESPN's game-level summary. Keep the 1999–2025 comparison definitions and eligibility unchanged.

## Implementation contract, revision 2

Store optional `facts` on each existing v1 snap. Optional members are `timeouts` (a Jets, opponent pair), `goalToGo`, `kind` (completion, incompletion, interception, rush, sack, kneel or spike), `yards`, `firstDown`, `touchdown`, `simpleAdvance`, `passer`, `target`, `rusher`, `interceptor`, `epa`, `dropbackProbability`, `completionProbability`, `airYards` and `afterCatch`. Omit absent values. Validate probability, coordinate and timeout ranges, and preserve zero and negative values. Translate the home/away timeout pair to Jets/opponent order at extraction.

A down, distance, possession and field position on an ordinary scrimmage action are required for a schematic. Special teams, conversions, nullified plays and ambiguous penalties do not get the graphic. Only a verified uncomplicated run or completion can place the finish. A goal-to-go line ends at the goal line. Mark model EPA with the source offense. Distinguish the model's pre-snap dropback tendency from its completion estimate on the throw. Omit unavailable model metrics. For the magnitude rank, count only non-null Jets deltas in this game's prefix, including the selected snap. Use competition rank (one plus the number strictly larger), mark a tie at the source precision and omit rank below half a percentage point or with fewer than two observations.

The odds readout can pair the current snap’s pre-play estimate with the same source row’s directly reported Jets post-play value, using the existing optional `postWp` field. An ESPN score gap may show a clearly labeled last-recorded score from the game prefix. Wilson’s existing film study supplies charted coverage, rush count and release time for its one matched play. The play reader names the different source editions when linking their EPA estimates.
